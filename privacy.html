Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our top priority. Learn how we protect and handle your health data.

🛡️

100% Private & Secure

Your data stays on your device. We never share, sell, or analyze your personal health information.

📄

Information We Collect

  • Blood pressure readings (systolic, diastolic, pulse)
  • Medication information (name, dosage, frequency)
  • Medication log entries and timestamps
  • Optional notes you add to readings
  • App settings and preferences
💾

How We Store Your Data

  • All data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework
  • Data is encrypted using industry-standard encryption
  • Optional iCloud sync keeps your data synchronized across your Apple devices
  • iCloud data is encrypted both in transit and at rest
  • You maintain full control over your iCloud data
✨

How We Use Your Data

  • Display your health trends and statistics within the app
  • Generate personalized insights about your blood pressure patterns
  • Send you reminder notifications (if enabled)
  • Create exportable reports for sharing with healthcare providers
  • Your data is NEVER used for advertising or marketing
🚫

Data Sharing

  • We DO NOT share your data with third parties
  • We DO NOT sell your data to anyone
  • We DO NOT use your data for analytics or tracking
  • Data only leaves your device when you explicitly export it
  • iCloud sync is handled entirely by Apple's secure infrastructure
👤

Your Rights

  • Access all your data at any time within the app
  • Export your data in PDF or CSV format
  • Delete individual readings or medications
  • Clear all data permanently from Settings
  • Disable iCloud sync at any time in device settings
🔒

Security Measures

  • Data encryption using Apple's security frameworks
  • Secure local storage with device-level protection
  • No external servers or databases
  • App runs entirely on-device
  • Face ID/Touch ID support for app access (optional)

Last Updated: January 2026

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the date above and posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
